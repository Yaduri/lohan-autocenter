# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework), Lucide icons inlined as an SVG sprite. Confirmed by the user.

## Users
Drivers in Campo Limpo Paulista, Várzea Paulista, Jundiaí, Jarinu and Franco da Rocha (25–60, car used for work and family; also app drivers, small fleets, and women who felt pushed or deceived at other workshops). They arrive mostly on mobile from Google or Instagram, often with a symptom (car pulling, wheel shaking, noise) and no name for the problem.

## Product Purpose
Lohan Auto Center is a multibrand auto center. The landing page exists to generate WhatsApp quote requests that already carry Name, Car/Year and City. Success = WhatsApp clicks and form submits.

## Positioning
"The workshop that explains before it touches your car." Backed by 4.9 ★ from 343 Google reviews and a 3D alignment rig that shows readings on screen.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Core: 3D alignment, balancing, suspension, tyres (domestic and imported). Complementary: oil and filters, brakes, clutch, general mechanics.
- Never mention electrical work, battery, injection, scanner, alternator, starter or air-conditioning anywhere.
- Hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00, Sat 08:00–12:00, Sun closed.
- Open items: tyre brand list, service times in FAQ, whether the 3D report is handed to the customer.

## Brand Commitments
Logo: SUV outline in black line + "LOHAN" in orange→red gradient. Palette and type pinned in direcao-visual.md (warm white base, Lohan orange #E0602E / action #C24A26, graphite #1C1917; Archivo + IBM Plex Mono). Voice: the trusted mechanic who explains; short sentences, "você", no empty superlatives.

## Evidence on Hand
Real photos (img/): façade, alignment lift with pickup, lift with sedan, workshop with lifts, tyre racks. Real Google reviews: Manoel Alves, Saulo Marçal, Nileia Nunes. No team photos, no 3D panel screenshot: panel values on the page are illustrative and labelled as such.

## Product Principles
1. Explain, never push.
2. One action everywhere: WhatsApp with a prefilled message.
3. Only real photos and real reviews.
4. Mobile first; the first screen shows the headline and the first form field.
